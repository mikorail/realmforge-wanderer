# Realmforge Wanderer

Caves-of-Qud-style ASCII roguelike played across worlds made in Realmforge (`../realmforge`). Separate app: Realmforge only exports world files (Export dialog → "Wanderer World", or `npx tsx scripts/adventure-world.ts` there); this game loads them. No LLM/AI-model API calls.

## Stack
- Go 1.23, no deps (module `wanderer`): `engine/` is the whole game and draws a 112×34 character grid; `cmd/wasm` exposes `window.qud` (`palette`, `init(worldJSON)` → chargen options, `newGame(spec)`, `load(save)`, `key(k)`, `save()`, `meta()`, `deeds()`).
- Web shell: Vite 8 + Svelte 5 runes (no SvelteKit), `fflate`, Share Tech Mono. `src/App.svelte` (title: continue runs, bundled sample worlds, load a Realmforge .json), `CharGen.svelte` (species → calling → point-buy → genre-named powers/defects → origin; rules mirror `engine/chargen.go`), `Play.svelte` (canvas grid, keys incl. numpad, click-to-walk, autosave every 150 keys), `qud.ts` (wasm loader), `store.ts` (IndexedDB `realmforge-wanderer`: meta/save/world per world seed, deflated).

- `public/worlds/` holds bundled sample worlds + `index.json` (regenerate from Realmforge: `npx tsx scripts/adventure-world.ts <years> <genre> <seed> <template> 3`; samples are 3-planet `wanderer-universe` files).
- Tests: `go test ./engine/` (scenario tests against the bundled worlds: combat, death, ritual, quests, trade, sky falls, breath, powers, stairs, travel, save/load, every species × calling).
- Browser smoke test: `npm run smoke` (`scripts/smoke.mjs`, ≈30 s with build, ≈15 s with `-- --no-build`): vite build + wasm straight into `dist/wasm` (the committed `public/wasm/qud.wasm` is never touched), `vite preview` on a free port (5197 first), then headless Chromium (global `playwright`): title, `qud.init` on every bundled world, CharGen → Play, C/o/j (all journal tabs incl. ## Engine map (`engine/`)
`world.go` WorldData schema (must match Realmforge `src/lib/adventure/export.ts`) · `genre.go` per-genre `Skin` (callings, power names, materials, gear, ritual, currency) · `zone.go` 80×25 zones, 3×3 per parasang; z>0 strata and Realmforge deep layers, z=−1 sky isles, sea floor; towns with the sim's notables, ruins with chronicle inscriptions, crypts of dead rulers holding their relics, wonders, sim-monster lairs · `terrain.go` · `creatures.go` · `items.go` · `entity.go` (body plans, limbs) · `combat.go` (d20+AGI vs DV, up to 3 penetration rolls PV vs AV chaining, dismember/bleed/stun, elements, fire) · `ai.go` (shadowcast FOV, light/darkvision, BFS pursuit) · `faction.go` (rep per realm/faith/creature faction from home realm relations and wars; NPC generation) · `talk.go` (ritual → rep + secret from history, gossip from real events, trade, quests, ferries, lore books) · `powers.go` · `input.go` · `render.go` · `game.go` (turn/energy loop, survival, world map, save/load, deeds, `MusicState` for the soundtrack).
- `city.go`: towns ranked by a size score (`WorldData.cityTier`, top 8% → 3×3 great cities, next 25% → 5 zones); each zone is a district picked from the town's real economic sectors (market, harbor, temple + graveyard of real dead, academy, barracks, arena, forge, artisans, mines, farms, slums, homes, palace/plaza), walls/gates on edges facing open country. Great cities and tiered capitals also sprawl into **outer wards** (`cityRing`/`ringAt`, `cityPlan.ring` keyed by zone offset −1..3 from the city parasang): the 16 zones bordering the 3×3 inside the neighbour parasangs (land, not mountains, no town or wonder, not bordering another sprawling city) become unwalled districts (corners farms, gate sides outer markets, else homes/farms/artisans/forge), making a 5×5 city with the old walls inside; `districtAt` falls back to `ringAt`, so sites and zone generation agree. Overland travel is at true scale in `travel.go` (`tv`): a parasang is `WorldData.KmPer` km (Realmforge `kmPer`, default 160), a world-map step takes `tvMinutes` (km ÷ pace by biome/road/mountain/wings/Survival: days), `tvJourney` eats and drinks from the pack, the world map shows km and days (`tvInfo`). `landmarks.go`: palaces named after the ruling house, clock/bell/pagoda towers, bridges over rivers, universities, colosseums, columns of real dead rulers, lighthouses, cathedrals… often holding a unique item.
- `dungeon.go`: one deterministic `siteAt` registry (ruin catacombs, temple catacombs, mines, thieves' warrens, Realmforge outlaw hideouts, tower wonders, the Spire of Trials and Bottomless Abyss — placed by `WorldData.placeMegas` when the world file has none — plus seeded wilderness dungeons/towers). Dungeon levels are z>0 under the entrance zone; tower floors are z = −100−floor (`towerStair` keeps consecutive floors' stairs aligned). Themes, bosses, locked vaults + keys, hidden traps (`s` searches), ore.
- `cooking.go`: `C` cooks at a campfire / town hearth (no hostiles in sight, not in water). Ingredients (`Kind "ingredient"`, or a beast corpse; `Item.Effect` = one of 10 tags: hearty spry thick mending keen ember rime deep lasting venom) come from kills (`corpseTag`: flags fire/frost/aquatic/fly/…; people and undead give none), wild herbs and cave fungi (`scatterHerbs`, side rng `seed:herbs:zoneKey`, so zone generation is unchanged) and merchants' local staples parsed from `CultureData.Food` (`staples`/`stapleTag`). 2 ingredients (3 at Survival 2) → `Actor.Meal` tag→level (max 2) for `Game.MealLeft` minutes (1440 + 360 × Survival, +25% for a known dish); hooks in `Score`/`AV`/`DV`, regen, `breathCap`, `hurt` (`resistMeal`), `onHit` (venom), hunger (`slowHunger`). New combos go into `Game.Cookbook`, named after the local culture; journal tab 4. `applyMeal` shares a cooked meal with companions in the zone (`Actor.Meal` + `fed` status via `feedCompanion`, cleared when it expires; +3 loyalty at most every 6 h, `Companion.MealAt`). Companions also accept a `dish` (meal tags, +10 loyalty) or a raw ingredient (+2). Merchants also sell a culture's signature `dish` (`localDish`: two staples, deterministic per culture) that gives a meal without a fire. Kin (after the ritual) offer "Swap recipes" once (`fl recipes`): they teach their dish, you teach one from another land for reputation. Tests: `cooking_test.go`.
- `companions.go`: recruited followers keep a `Companion` record (`Actor.Comp`: original faction/AI, home zone + town, kin names, loyalty 0..100, XP, kills, guard post) and a standing order (`Actor.Order`: "" follow, stay, guard, attack, distance, passive). `o` opens the companions screen (list, HP, loyalty, order, who waits where; orders for all; attack uses the target cursor, `Pend "order:<id>"`). Talking/bumping a stationary follower opens companion talk (orders, give gear → worn if `itemScore` is better, food/tonics raise loyalty, take items back, about themselves, mood, part ways → `releaseCompanion` sends them home with their old faction). `companionAct` (hook in `aiAct`) runs the orders and never targets their own realm (`ownPeople`). Kills grant companion XP (killer full, helpers within 8 half) → level ups. Loyalty drifts hourly with `g.Rep` of their realm/faith and famine, moves with `AddRep` (`compRep`); below 10 they leave. Stay/guard companions remain in their zone (`compComes` in `enterZone`); others follow across edges, stairs and world travel. Deaths become `companion` deeds. Powers-made followers get a `Summoned` record (orders, no loyalty). Tests: `companions_test.go`.
- Companions 2 (`compbanter.go` + `cbdata.go` `cb`, `compquest.go` `cq`, `comppowers.go` `cp`; tests `companions2_test.go`). **Banter**: enter hook (home town, wonder/ruin once each via `Seen`, enemy-realm town, temple = priest of their faith, own realm), turn hook (weather change, feast day, shared meal / night camp via `Companion.MealAt`, level-up, near death, grumble at loyalty < 30, pairs: kin / rival realms / generic + reply), kill hook (one-liner). Lines in `cbLines` keyed by trigger, `Tag` filter (old/tech, `g:<genre>`, `sp:<species>`, high/low caste, kin, faith), placeholders `{me} {you} {town} {kin} {realm} {faith}…`; picked by hash (never `g.R`). Cooldowns: `cbGap` per companion, `cbGlobal` overall, nothing but one-liners (`cbQuip`) while foes are near. State `g.ext("cb")`. **Personal quests**: hourly, a recruited companion with loyalty ≥ 70 after 3 days offers one (`cqMake`, side rng `seed:cq:id`; one open at a time, one per companion): avenge (named bandit/beast near home), heirloom (nearest ruin or wilds), peace (estranged relative in a nearby town), debt (creditor at home). Accept/decline in companion talk (`cqTalkOptions`, one line in `companionTalk`); mirrored into `g.Quests` as Kind `companion` (journal + map, inert for core quest code); targets placed by enter hook, finished by kill hook / held item / talk hook on the NPC; reward +30 loyalty, perk (attr/HP/AV/DV), player XP, `companion` deed; fails if the companion leaves. State `g.ext("cq")`. **Powers**: `cpCast` (one line at the top of `companionAct`) uses augment and natural powers with rules (heal self < 40% or adjacent player < 35%; flames/freeze/lightning/arcanebolt on the foe with most neighbours, clear line, blast sparing allies and bystanders; leech/syphon when hurt; sprint/phase to flee when passive or < 25%; forcebubble when surrounded; stunforce with the player out of range); cooldowns in the companion's `Actor.Cool`, ticked by `cpTick`.
- `weather.go`: seasons (`DayOf`/`SeasonOfDay`: 4 × 28-day seasons, game opens in spring) and `WorldData.TempAt` (parasang `Temp` + seasonal swing, larger for cold/high/continental, + diurnal). `WorldData.WeatherAt(p, turn)` is a pure schedule: smooth noise over (4-hour bucket, 4×4-parasang region) read through biome humidity/temperature → clear, overcast, rain, storm (lightning on tall terrain/exposed actors), snow, blizzard, fog, sandstorm, ash fall, spore drift, prism haze, heat, frost, plus genre swaps (`genreWeather`: acid rain, smog, coal fog, fallout, spore rain, pollen, tempest). All text/effects in the `weatherKinds` table. Never draws `g.R` (side hashes of seed+turn+cell). Applies only outdoors (`outdoors`: z 0, not dark/underwater/sky) and off roofed tiles (`roofed`: floor/carpet/bed…). Hooks: FOV cap in `recompute`, missile penalty in `shoot`, move cost/slipping in `stepTo`, `weatherTick` in `turn()` (change messages via saved `Game.WxLast`, dousing, cold hunger / heat thirst, acid sting, lightning), `travelWeather` in `WorldMove`, side-panel `weatherLine`, world-map `weatherInfo`, map overlay `overlayTile` in `drawMap`, `Music.Wx`, `Music.Season`, `Music.Bolt` (strike counter bumped in `lightning`; the web player plays thunder when it rises). Tests: `weather_test.go` (`Game.wxForce` pins the weather).
- `augments.go`: body augments (20 `AugDefs`, one mechanic skinned per genre in `augSkins`: runic tattoos, blessed grafts, clockwork prosthetics, servo rigs, isotope implants, cyberware, symbiotes, photosynthetic lattices). Installed into `BodyPart.Aug` (one per part; slots head/face/body/back/hand/feet, feet also fits a tail), costing capacity `3 + Mod(base TOU) + level/5` (min 1). Effects are read live through hooks in `Score`/`AV`/`DV`/`Quick`/`LightRadius`/`Pow` (granted powers, active ones listed by `Abilities` via `augAbilities`)/`Weapons` (natural weapon in an empty hand)/`hurt` (`augResist`), so removal restores at once and a severed limb silences its augment until regrown or healed. Installers (role `augmenter`, genre title) sit in one district per tier ≥1 city (`augInstallerDistricts`), placed after `assignSociety` with side rng `seed:aug:zoneKey`; talk → `augMenu` (install from pack, buy and install, remove for half fee). Rare ones (reflex, flamer, cloak, wings) only drop from bosses / locked vaults (`augLoot`, side rng). Purist faiths (animism, pantheism, ancestral) overcharge and remark on load ≥ 4; esoteric/liberation faiths give 8% off. Character sheet tab 3. Companions take augments too: `augMenuFor(installer, who)` ("Work on X instead" in the installer menu, or companion talk "Let the … fit you" when an installer is in the zone), capacity from their own TOU/level; augments stay in their body through save/load and `releaseCompanion`. Tests: `augments_test.go`, `polish_test.go`.
- Agent-built systems: `rarity.go` (common→unique tiers, biblical/occult/omni/… categories, boss drops), `powers2.go` (swimming, diving, climbing, clipping, spells, clairvoyance, time dilation, force bubble, fugue, multiple arms…), `inventory.go` (weight, backpacks, encumbrance, tabbed sorted inventory with compare), `attire.go` (regional clothes per sphere, tailors, transmog, attire affinity), `checks.go` (Disco/Fallout skill checks + inner voices), `society.go` (strata per sphere/doctrine/ideology/genre, households, kin, caste prices/greetings). Web: `src/garden.ts` (default soundtrack outside combat, setting `musicStyle` auto/drift/pastoral/desert: **drift** = Eno/C418 incommensurate loops (4-5 voices, periods 17-37 s), rubato felt-piano phrases, low pad, planned 25-60 s silences; **pastoral** = Stardew 4/4 fingerpicking + A A B A motif melody + shaker, band per season, culture-sphere leads; **desert** = Qud modal drones through a breathing filter, ornamented synth/oud phrases, glass bells, odd-length analog arp, maqsum hand drum in the open; `gardenFor` picks one by mood/biome/genre for `auto`; scenes are seeded per place and cross-fade; `LEVEL` matched to about -26 dB by offline render; `settings.ts` migrates old saved 'chiptune' to 'auto' once via `musicRev`), `src/music.ts` (procedural adaptive chiptune from `frame.music`, still plays combat and the chiptune/ambient styles; `WX` table colours it per weather kind: rain patter, thunder, snow sparse/high, fog drone, sand/ash wash, heat tempo; season shifts key + mode via `pieceKey`), `src/settings.ts` + `Settings.svelte` (⚙ / backquote).
- `planets.go` (`un`): a world file is a single world or a `wanderer-universe` (`{kind, start, planets:[world…], rifts:[{id,name,a:{planet,burg},b:{planet,burg}}]}`); `ParseWorld` returns the start planet, every planet points at the shared `Universe` (`WorldData.uni/uniIdx`; a lone world = universe of 1). **Per-planet isolation by swapping**: the Game always holds the current planet in its usual fields; stepping through a rift (`UnGo`) moves Zones, Known, Slain, Taken, Visited, Read, FeastSeen/Note, Rep, HomeState/Faith, Start*, Quests, Arena*, WxLast, the planet-local ext stores (`unLocalKeys`: bh cu home orders pl spine uw war ws vl), the zone-keyed parts of `in` (Spots/Zones/Enc) and `lq` (Foraged) and murim's sect half (`unMr`: membership, missions, raids, tournament, rep baselines; cultivation and techniques stay global) into an `unSnap` in `g.ext("uni")`, then restores the destination's (or `unFresh`: stranger there, state:/faith:/mr: rep reset, creature rep kept, Start = arrival burg). `g.Seed`/`Genre`/`Sk` follow the planet; `Meta().seed` stays the home planet's (`unHome`) so the shell files the run under one key. Saves never hold worlds: `LoadGame` picks the saved planet out of the world file (`unResolve`); old saves (no "uni") load on the start planet. New planet-local state keyed by zone/burg/realm/person ids must go into `unLocalKeys` (or its own ext key added there). Rift gates: terrain `TPortal` 'Ø' in the town zone of each rift burg (surface, deep layer, sea floor or sky: `unTownZone`), placed by a zone + enter hook; bump → mode "rift" (y/letter/Esc) → "You step through X onto Y". World map 'Ø' + info lines, sidebar planet name, `Music.Planet` (planet kind), `Deed.Planet`. `FuzzRift` is used by `cmd/fuzz`. Tests: `planets_test.go`.
- `zwildlife.go` (named to sort last so its zone hook runs after every other) + `bestiary_*.go` (`bs`): Earth's bestiary across deep time, 854 species (living, recently extinct, Pleistocene, Cenozoic, Mesozoic, Paleozoic, Ediacaran) as one-line data rows `name|group|when|habitat|size|diet|temper|activity|flags|glyph+colour|rarity|attack|desc` parsed at init into `bsSp` + `CTpl` "bs_<slug>" registered in `tplByID` only (never `Templates`, so `pickCreature` is unchanged). Zone hook `bsZone` (file sorts last, so it runs after every other zone hook; side rng `seed:bs:zone:key`, append only): wildlife by biome, height (`mtn`), temperature (warm/cold seas, `ice`), coast/rivers, caves, sky, sea surface and sea floor; predators (`predator`/`pack`) are faction `beasts`, the rest faction `wildlife` (not hostile until provoked); punk genres get occasional `paApplyMorph` variants; Pleistocene megafauna roam cold steppes in fantasy/mythic/wasteland/twilight. Rare lost worlds (`bsLostAt`, pure hash of seed + parasang): Lost Plateau (dinosaurs, tropical/wet), Hidden Valley (Pleistocene, cold/steppe), Primordial Cavern (z 2..8, Paleozoic/Triassic), Abyssal Trench (open sea floor, marine reptiles and old fishes); tide-pool Precambrian/Paleozoic curiosities on 12% of sea floors. Fossils (`Kind "fossil"`, `Effect "bs:<id>"`, encyclopedia entry as Lore) in strata, underdark, ruins, badlands and lost worlds; collectors (bookseller, naturalist, chronicler, academy zones) buy them at full value and tell of the nearest lost world. `bsAct` (one line in `aiAct`): nocturnal/diurnal sleep, skittish flight, herds alarm together, territorial warning then charge, predators hunt smaller wildlife. Kills yield meat (cooking tag), hide/pelt, bones, horns/antlers, ivory, feathers, shell, carapace (`bsProducts`). Codex: seen wildlife and fossils are recorded with period lines ("Late Cretaceous, ~68 million years ago"). `bsSpecies(name)` for other systems (mounts). State `g.ext("bs")`. Tests: `bestiary_test.go`.
- `slain.go` (`sl`): monsters with `slain` (+`slainYear`, `slainBy`) never spawn; `placeSimMonster` hands them to `slPlaceRemains` (bones, a trophy relic, an inscription "Here fell X, slain in Y by Z"; town zones stay towns). Half of them (`slHasBrood`, hash of seed+id) leave a "Brood of X" (~60% stats, flag `brood`) in the lair; bounty boards post broods (`bhBounty.Kind "brood"`); killing it sets `g.Slain[m.ID]`. Secrets/rumours speak of the dead, quest spine and live bounties skip slain ones.
- `villains.go` (`vl`): living Role "villain" people, topped up to 2–4 per planet from criminals/traitors/cruel schemers (`bhCriminal`) then the merely cruel (`vlList`, cached per world). Each gets a hideout dungeon (theme hideout, depth 2) in a free sub-zone of their town's parasang (`vlSite` hook in `findSite`); `vlEnsure` turns the deepest hall's boss into the villain (+retinue, guaranteed `bossDrop`), they are removed from their town, townsfolk offer "Ask about X" (reveals the hideout), bounty boards post them (`vlBounty` in `bhGenPerson`, `bhPlaceTarget` reuses the villain), death → `villain` deed + realm rep. State `g.ext("vl")` (planet-local).
- `calendar.go`: local calendars and feasts from Realmforge (`CultureData.Months/Feasts/Festivals/Music/Architecture/Values`, `StateData.Feasts`, `FeastData{name,kind,month,text}`; all omitempty, old worlds fall back to `defaultMonths` + 3 generic `fallbackFeasts`). A feast's day in its month = hash(seed, culture, name), never `g.R`. Town feasts = realm's when the realm shares the town's culture, else the culture's. Side panel `dateLine` ("4th of Mango Blossom Y221", calendar of the town/land/home culture) + feast name; on a feast day in a town: `feastAnnounce` on entering and at dawn (`calendarTick`, once per town per day via `Game.FeastNote`), `feastTile` decorations in `drawMap` (garlands/lanterns/bonfires per kind, wall bunting), `feastGreeting`/`feastGossip` (today or within 14 days, with sphere festival lore) in `talk.go`, talk option "Join the …" (`joinFeast`: once per town+feast+year in `Game.FeastSeen`; free shared meal = `localDish` tags + the kind's tag, a festive dish to carry, +15 realm rep, 10 XP, entry in `Game.Feasts`), merchants stock the festive dish (`feastStock` in `openTrade`). Journal tab 5 Calendar (`calendarLines`). Tests: `calendar_test.go`.
